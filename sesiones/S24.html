What does a data analyst do?

What does a data analyst do? The short answer: analysts help companies make better decisions with data.

First, analysts collect data from different sources: sales systems, spreadsheets, websites or surveys. The data is usually messy, so the next step is cleaning it. Analysts remove duplicates, fix errors and check missing values. This part takes a lot of time, but it's very important.

Then they analyze the data. They calculate totals, averages and percentages, and they look for trends. For example, are sales going up or down? Which products are popular?

After that, analysts visualize the results in charts and dashboards. Tools like Excel, Power BI and Tableau are very common. Many analysts also use SQL and Python.

Finally, and this is the key part, they communicate insights to stakeholders, like managers or other teams. A good analyst explains the numbers in a simple way and answers one question: what can the company do now?
