2 Concepto: preposiciones con cifras

En la sesión 39 dijiste Sales increased sharply. Hoy agregas la cifra exacta. Cada preposición tiene un trabajo distinto:

Preposición Ejemplo
by
la diferencia (cuánto cambió) 		Sales increased by 5%.
from … to
valor inicial → valor final 		Users rose from 20,000 to 30,000.
to
solo el valor final		 Costs fell to $9,000.
of
con sustantivo: an increase of 		There was an increase of 5%.
at
un nivel: peak at, stable at 		Traffic peaked at 70,000 visits.
between … and
un rango		 Prices were between $20 and $25.

⚠️ El error típico: «aumentó en un 5%» → increased by 5% (nunca «in 5%»). Pero con sustantivo cambia: an increase of 5%.

Aproximar

Palabra Ejemplo
about / around
aproximadamente 		about 1,200 orders
nearly / almost
casi (un poco menos) 		nearly 30%
over / more than
más de		 over $50,000
under / less than
menos de 		under 5%
a total of
un total de		 a total of 4,400 orders

3 Escucha y repite

Tom resume el trimestre con cifras exactas. Escucha cada línea en normal, luego en lento, y repítela en voz alta tres veces imitando el ritmo.

Todo el texto:

  • Revenue increased by 25% in Q2.Los ingresos aumentaron un 25% en el segundo trimestre.
  • It rose from $120,000 to $150,000.Subieron de 120,000 a 150,000 dólares.
  • We had a total of 4,400 orders, an increase of 10%.Tuvimos un total de 4,400 pedidos, un aumento del 10%.
  • The return rate fell to 4%.La tasa de devoluciones bajó al 4%.
  • Traffic peaked at nearly 90,000 visits in June.El tráfico llegó a su máximo, con casi 90,000 visitas, en junio.
  • Prices remained stable at around $34.Los precios se mantuvieron estables en unos 34 dólares.

💡 Las preposiciones suenan débiles: by «bai» corto, from «frəm», to «tə». Las cifras llevan el acento.

5 Lectura corta

Un párrafo típico de un reporte de ventas, con cifras exactas. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

Q2 highlights

Q2 was our best quarter so far. Revenue increased by 25%, from $120,000 in Q1 to $150,000.

We had a total of 4,400 orders, an increase of 10%. The average order also went up, from $30 to $34.

The return rate fell to 4%, the lowest level of the year.

Website traffic peaked at nearly 90,000 visits in June, and about 60% of the visits came from mobile phones.

Marketing costs remained stable at around $8,000 per month. In Q3, we're going to focus on the mobile experience.
