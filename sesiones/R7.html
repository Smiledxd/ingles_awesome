4 Lectura corta

Regional sales: quick summary

Hi Sofía, here's a quick summary of regional sales for 2026.

Overall, it was a good year. Total sales increased by about 8%.

The Center region had the highest sales again: $132,000, 10% higher than in 2025. But the North region grew the most: sales rose sharply, from $80,000 to $96,000, an increase of 20%.

The South was the only region with a decrease. Its sales fell slightly, to $57,000. The East remained stable at $40,000.

The main takeaway: the North has the best potential. I think we need to look at it first. — Tom
