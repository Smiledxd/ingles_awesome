2 Concepto: was / were

El pasado de to be tiene solo dos formas: was y were. Traduce «era, estaba, fui, estuve».

Sujeto Pasado Presente
I / he / she / it was am / is
you / we / they were are
Ejemplo
Afirmativo I was at home. They were at the office.
Negativo I wasn't tired. We weren't late.
Pregunta Were you busy? Was the meeting long?
Respuesta Yes, I was. / No, it wasn't.

💡 Palabras para el pasado: yesterday (ayer), last night (anoche), last weekend (el fin de semana pasado). En la sesión 29 verás más.

Lugares y situaciones

  • I was at home last night.Anoche estuve en casa.
  • She was at work yesterday.Ayer ella estuvo en el trabajo.
  • We were in class from 8 to 10.Estuvimos en clase de 8 a 10.
  • They were on vacation last week.La semana pasada estuvieron de vacaciones.
  • The office was very crowded.La oficina estaba muy llena.
  • I was born in Arequipa in 2003.Nací en Arequipa en 2003.

⚠️ «Nací» es I was born (nunca «I born»). Y en inglés no hay diferencia entre «era» y «fui»: todo es was / were.

3 Escucha y repite

Lunes en la oficina: Diego le pregunta a Carla por su fin de semana. Escucha cada línea en normal, luego en lento, y repítela en voz alta tres veces imitando el ritmo.

Todo el texto:

  • A: Hi, Carla! Where were you on Saturday?¡Hola, Carla! ¿Dónde estuviste el sábado?
  • B: I was at a birthday party in Barranco.Estuve en una fiesta de cumpleaños en Barranco.
  • A: Was it fun?¿Estuvo divertida?
  • B: Yes, it was! The music was great, but the place was very crowded.¡Sí! La música estuvo genial, pero el lugar estaba muy lleno.
  • A: Were your friends from the university there?¿Estaban tus amigos de la universidad?
  • B: No, they weren't. They were in class.No. Estaban en clase.
  • A: And where were you last night? You weren't online.¿Y dónde estuviste anoche? No estabas conectada.
  • B: I was at home. I was very tired!Estuve en casa. ¡Estaba muy cansada!

💡 Was y were suenan débiles en la frase: «wəz», «wər». En las respuestas cortas suenan fuertes: Yes, I WAS.

5 Lectura corta

Carla escribe en su diario sobre un lunes difícil. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

A long Monday

Yesterday was a long day. I was at the office from 8 a.m. to 7 p.m.

The morning was difficult. The internet was very slow, and the sales file wasn't ready. My manager, Sofía, wasn't in the office. She was at a meeting with a client in San Isidro.

In the afternoon, Diego and Tom were very helpful. We were in the meeting room for three hours with the new dashboard.

At 7 p.m., I was really tired, but I was happy. The dashboard was finally ready!
