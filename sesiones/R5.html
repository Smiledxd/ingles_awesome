4 Lectura corta

Tom cuenta cómo llegó a trabajar en datos. Junta todo el pasado de la semana. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

From teacher to analyst

I was born in Texas, and I studied math at a small college. After college, I was a math teacher for three years.

I liked teaching, but I wasn't happy. Four years ago, I took an online course in SQL, and I loved it. I didn't know anything about data before that course!

Two years ago, I moved to Lima with my wife. I didn't speak Spanish very well, so I took classes every night. Last year, I got a job at DataLab as a junior analyst.

Last week my manager asked me, "Do you miss teaching?" I said, "Sometimes. But now I teach Excel to my coworkers!"
