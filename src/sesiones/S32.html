2 Concepto: will

Will + verbo base es igual para todas las personas (I will, she will). Al hablar se contrae: I'll, you'll, she'll. El negativo es won't (will not).

Tres usos

Uso Ejemplo
Decisión en el momento The phone is ringing. — I'll get it!
Ofrecer ayuda o prometer I'll help you with the report. I won't forget.
Predicción u opinión I think AI will change a lot of jobs.

going to vs. will

going to will
Plan decidido antes Decisión ahora, al hablar
I'm going to take a Python course. (ya me inscribí)
 Python? Good idea. I'll take a course!

Predicciones con opinión

  • I think companies will need data analysts.Creo que las empresas necesitarán analistas de datos.
  • I don't think AI will replace analysts.No creo que la IA reemplace a los analistas. (se niega think)
  • She'll probably get the job.Probablemente ella consiga el trabajo. (probably después de will)
  • It probably won't be easy.Probablemente no será fácil. (probably antes de won't)
  • Maybe I'll work abroad in the future.Quizás trabaje en el extranjero en el futuro.

💡 I'll suena «ail», casi como I con una «l» al final. Won't suena «wount» (no confundir con want «uánt»).

5 Lectura corta

Un artículo corto sobre el futuro de los trabajos de datos. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

Data jobs in 2030

What will data jobs look like in 2030? Nobody knows for sure, but experts agree on some ideas.

First, companies will have a lot of data, and they will need people to understand it. Data analysts won't disappear.

Second, AI tools will do many simple tasks, like cleaning data or writing basic queries. Analysts will probably spend their time on questions and decisions, not on simple tasks.

Third, communication skills will be very important. A good analyst will explain results to managers in a simple way.

So, what can you do today? Learn the basics, practice with real data, and don't be afraid of AI. It will be your assistant, not your boss.
