2 Concepto: presente perfecto

El presente perfecto es «he trabajado, he aprendido». Se forma con have / has + participio. En una entrevista es clave: con él hablas de tu experiencia sin decir una fecha exacta, y de lo que has hecho hasta ahora.

Estructura
✅ I / you / we / they have worked
he / she / it has worked
❌ I haven't used Python.
She hasn't finished.
❓ Have you ever used Power BI?
Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

💡 Contracciones: I have → I've, she has → she's, have not → haven't. Ojo: she's puede ser she is o she has; lo sabes por el verbo que sigue (she's worked = she has worked).

Ejemplos

  • I have worked with Excel for two years.He trabajado con Excel durante dos años. (y sigo)
  • I've learned a lot of SQL this year.He aprendido mucho SQL este año.
  • She has used Power BI since March.Ella ha usado Power BI desde marzo.
  • I have never used Tableau.Nunca he usado Tableau.
  • Have you ever worked with real data? — Yes, I have.¿Alguna vez has trabajado con datos reales? — Sí.
  • I haven't finished the report yet.Todavía no he terminado el reporte.

Participios que más vas a usar

Los regulares terminan en -ed, igual que el pasado: work → worked, learn → learned, use → used, analyze → analyzed. Los irregulares hay que aprenderlos:

Verbo Pasado Participio
be was / were been
do did done
make made made
write wrote written
build built built
take took taken
see saw seen

Palabras que acompañan al presente perfecto

Palabra Uso
ever / never alguna vez / nunca (experiencia)
for two years durante un periodo
since March desde un momento
yet todavía (al final, en ❌ y ❓)
already ya (antes del participio)
so far / this year hasta ahora / este año

💡 Para preguntar cuánto tiempo: How long have you studied English? → For six months. / Since June.

3 Escucha y repite

En una entrevista, le preguntan a Diego por su experiencia. Escucha cada línea en normal, luego en lento, y repítela en voz alta tres veces imitando el ritmo.

Todo el texto:

  • A: Have you ever worked with real data?¿Alguna vez has trabajado con datos reales?
  • B: Yes, I have. I've analyzed sales data in two university projects.Sí. He analizado datos de ventas en dos proyectos de la universidad.
  • A: How long have you studied data analysis?¿Cuánto tiempo has estudiado análisis de datos?
  • B: I've studied it for about a year.Lo he estudiado durante un año, más o menos.
  • A: Have you ever used Python?¿Alguna vez has usado Python?
  • B: No, I haven't. But I've already started an online course.No. Pero ya empecé un curso en línea.

💡 En la conversación, have casi siempre se contrae y suena débil: I've «aiv», she's «shiz». En las respuestas cortas no se contrae: Yes, I have.

5 Lectura corta

Diego publica en LinkedIn un resumen de su año. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

My year in data

This year has been very important for me. I have learned more than in any other year, and I want to share it here.

I have finished three online courses: Excel, SQL and Power BI. I have built two dashboards for my university projects, and I have written my first Python scripts.

I have also practiced English every day since October. It has been hard, but I have never enjoyed studying so much.

I haven't worked as a data analyst yet. That's my goal for next year: I'm going to apply for internships in Lima.
