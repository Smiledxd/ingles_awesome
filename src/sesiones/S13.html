2 Concepto: there is / there are

Para decir «hay» se usa there is con singular y there are con plural. Es un error muy común traducir «hay» con have: «Have a park» o «It has a park» → There is a park.

Singular Plural
Afirmativo There is (There's) a park. There are two banks.
Negativo There isn't a gym. There are no hospitals.
Pregunta Is there a library? Are there any parks?
Respuesta Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.

💡 En la conversación casi siempre se contrae: there's («ders»). En plural no se contrae al escribir: there are. Con plurales en preguntas se usa any (lo vemos a fondo en la sesión 18).

¿Dónde? Tres preposiciones de lugar

  • There's a bank near my house.Hay un banco cerca de mi casa. (near = cerca de)
  • There's a café next to the library.Hay un café al lado de la biblioteca. (next to = al lado de)
  • There are three computer labs in the university.Hay tres laboratorios de cómputo en la universidad. (in = en, dentro de)
  • Is there a bus stop near here? — Yes, there is.¿Hay un paradero cerca de aquí? — Sí.
  • There are no parks in my street.No hay parques en mi calle.

💡 There's a se pronuncia muy unido: «dér-za». Repite el ritmo, no palabra por palabra.

5 Lectura corta

Carla describe su universidad. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

My university

My university is in Lima. It's big and it's very green. There are ten buildings and there's a big library in the center of the campus.

The library is my favorite place. There are big tables and there's a quiet room for students. It opens at 7 a.m. and it closes at 10 p.m.

There are three computer labs. The computers have Excel, Power BI and Python. I usually work there on Tuesday and Thursday.

There's a cafeteria next to the library. There isn't a gym, and there's no swimming pool. There's a bus stop near the main entrance. I take the bus to the university every day.
