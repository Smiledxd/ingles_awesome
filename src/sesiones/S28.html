2 Concepto: did en negativo y en preguntas

Es la misma idea que do / does del presente (sesión 9), pero en pasado: el auxiliar es did para todas las personas. La regla de oro: did ya está en pasado, así que el verbo principal vuelve a su forma base.

Estructura Ejemplo
Afirmativo verbo en pasado I went to Cusco.
Negativo didn't + verbo base I didn't go to Cusco.
Pregunta Did + sujeto + verbo base Did you go to Cusco?
Respuesta Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. Yes, I did.
Wh- Wh- + did + sujeto + verbo base Where did you stay?

⚠️ Errores típicos: «I didn't went» → I didn't go. «Did you saw…?» → Did you see…? Y con to be no se usa did: «Did you tired?» → Were you tired?

Preguntas típicas sobre un viaje

  • Where did you go?¿Adónde fuiste?
  • How did you travel? — By plane.¿Cómo viajaste? — En avión.
  • Where did you stay? — At a small hotel.¿Dónde te quedaste? — En un hotel pequeño.
  • What did you do there?¿Qué hiciste allá?
  • How was the trip? — It was amazing!¿Cómo estuvo el viaje? — ¡Increíble! (con to be: sin did)
  • I didn't visit the museum. We didn't have time.No visité el museo. No tuvimos tiempo.

Carla le pregunta a Diego por su viaje a Cusco. Escucha cada línea en normal, luego en lento, y repítela en voz alta tres veces imitando el ritmo.

Todo el texto:

  • A: Did you have a good trip, Diego?¿Tuviste un buen viaje, Diego?

5 Lectura corta

Una mini entrevista del boletín de la empresa a Sofía sobre sus vacaciones. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

Five questions for Sofía

Where did you go? — I went to Arequipa with my husband and my two kids.

How did you travel? — We flew from Lima. The flight was only one hour and a half.

What did you do there? — We walked around the historic center, we visited the Santa Catalina Monastery and we ate a lot of rocoto relleno.

Did you go to the Colca Canyon? — Yes, we did. We saw the condors, but my kids didn't like the early start at 3 a.m.!

Did you check your work email? — No, I didn't. Not once!
