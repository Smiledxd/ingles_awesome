Application: Data Analytics Intern

Dear Ms. Torres,

I'm writing to apply for the Data Analytics Intern position at RetailPlus. I'm a fifth-year industrial engineering student, and I'm going to finish my degree in July.

I have advanced Excel skills and basic knowledge of SQL. I'm currently working part-time at DataLab. There, I build dashboards in Power BI. I'd like to join your team because I'm very interested in retail data.

I'm available for an interview any day next week, except Tuesday morning. I'm meeting a client then.

Please find attached my CV. I'm sure I'll learn a lot with your team.

Best regards, Diego Soto
