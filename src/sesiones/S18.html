2 Concepto: contables e incontables

Los contables se pueden contar (one egg, two eggs): tienen plural y usan a / an. Los incontables no: no tienen plural ni a / an (rice, water, money).

Contables Incontables
an egg → eggs rice, bread
an apple → apples water, milk, coffee
a file → files money, time
a report → reports information, data, advice

⚠️ Para el trabajo: information y data son incontables en el uso diario. Nunca «informations» ni «an information»: some information, a lot of data.

some / any

Uso Ejemplo
some afirmativo I need some eggs. We have some rice.
any negativo y preguntas We don't have any milk. Are there any apples?

much / many / a lot of

Con Ejemplo
many contables (negativo, preguntas) How many eggs do we need?
much incontables (negativo, preguntas) We don't have much time.
a lot of los dos (sobre todo afirmativo) We have a lot of data.

Para preguntar precios: How much is it? / How much are they?

Ejemplos

  • We need some bread and some eggs.Necesitamos pan y huevos.
  • There isn't any coffee.No hay café.
  • How many customers are there in the file?¿Cuántos clientes hay en el archivo?
  • How much is the chicken? — It's twelve soles.¿Cuánto cuesta el pollo? — Doce soles.
  • This dataset has a lot of information.Este dataset tiene mucha información.

Carla y su compañera de departamento revisan qué comprar. Escucha cada línea en normal, luego en lento, y repítela en voz alta tres veces imitando el ritmo.

Todo el texto:

  • A: Do we have any milk?¿Tenemos leche?

  • A: How many eggs do we need?¿Cuántos huevos necesitamos?

💡 some en frases afirmativas suena débil y rápido: «səm». A lot of se une: «a-lá-dəv».

5 Lectura corta

Carla cuenta cómo hace las compras de la semana. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

Saturday shopping

Every Saturday morning, my roommate and I go to the supermarket near our apartment in Surco.

We check the kitchen before we go. We usually have some rice and some pasta. We never have any fresh fruit. We need a lot of eggs, because we cook eggs every morning.

At the supermarket, we don't buy many snacks. We don't have much money, and snacks are expensive. We buy chicken, vegetables, milk and bread.

I always keep the receipts in a spreadsheet. It shows how much money we spend every week. It's a small data project!
