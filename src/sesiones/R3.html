4 Lectura corta

Un texto que junta todo lo de la semana. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

A new intern at DataLab

DataLab is a small company in Miraflores. There are twelve people on the team, and there's a big meeting room with two screens.

Luis is the new intern. He can use Excel very well, and he can write simple SQL queries. He can't use Python yet. He loves solving problems, and he enjoys working with numbers.

On his first day, Sofía gives him some instructions: "Open the sales file. Filter the table by city. Don't delete any data, and save your work every hour."

The file has a lot of information: 4,300 rows and 15 columns. Total sales are $250,000.

100 palabras.

Comprensión
