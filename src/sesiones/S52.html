2 Concepto: who, which, that

Las relativas son el «que» de «una persona que analiza datos». Sirven para dar más información sobre un sustantivo, y son la forma natural de definir algo.

Pronombre Para…
who personas
which cosas
that personas y cosas (el más común en definiciones)

La fórmula para definir

Término + is a / an + categoría + who / that + qué hace.
A data analyst is a person who turns data into decisions.

  • A pivot table is a tool that summarizes data.Una tabla dinámica es una herramienta que resume datos.
  • A KPI is a number that measures performance.Un KPI es un número que mide el desempeño.
  • An outlier is a value that is very different from the others.Un valor atípico es un valor muy distinto de los demás.
  • A stakeholder is a person who needs the results of your analysis.Un stakeholder es una persona que necesita los resultados de tu análisis.
  • A slicer is a visual which filters the whole page.Un segmentador es un visual que filtra toda la página.
  • The intern who built this dashboard is from Lima.El practicante que hizo este dashboard es de Lima.

El error típico: repetir el sujeto

❌ Error ✅ Correcto
a report that it shows KPIs a report that shows KPIs
a person which analyzes data a person who analyzes data
a tool who cleans data a tool that cleans data

💡 who / which / that ya es el sujeto: no agregues it, he, she. Y el verbo concuerda con el sustantivo: a tool that summarizes (3.ª persona, con -s).

5 Lectura corta

El equipo de datos de una empresa en Lima prepara un glosario para los practicantes nuevos. Léelo primero sin audio, luego escúchalo y responde las preguntas.

Glossary for new interns

Welcome to the data team! Here are some terms that you will hear every day.

A KPI (key performance indicator) is a number that shows how well the business is doing. Monthly sales and the number of new customers are two KPIs which we track every week.

A stakeholder is a person who needs the results of our work. For example, the sales manager is a stakeholder who uses our dashboards to make decisions.

An outlier is a value that is very different from the others. Always check outliers before you calculate an average, because they can change it a lot.

A data pipeline is a process that moves data from one system to another. Our pipeline updates the dashboards every morning at 7 a.m.
